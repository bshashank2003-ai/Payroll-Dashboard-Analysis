# 💰 Payroll Dashboard Analysis | HR Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Payroll Dashboard Analysis** project focuses on analyzing employee payroll data to understand salary distribution, gross earnings, net pay, employer costs, statutory deductions, bonuses, compensation trends, and pay equity.

This project uses an interactive Excel dashboard to transform raw payroll data into meaningful business insights. It helps HR teams and management monitor payroll expenses, understand compensation patterns, and make informed workforce-related decisions.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The dashboard provides an overview of payroll performance through KPI cards, trend analysis, and data breakdowns.

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks the following KPIs:

- **Total Employer Cost:** Overall cost incurred by the organization for employee compensation.
- **Gross Earnings:** Total employee earnings before deductions.
- **Net Pay:** Final take-home salary paid to employees.
- **TDS Deducted:** Total income tax deducted at source.
- **Statutory & Retiral Cost:** Employer contributions towards PF, ESI, and gratuity provision.
- **Bonus as % of Gross:** Percentage of gross earnings allocated to bonuses and incentives.
- **Average Monthly Fixed Gross:** Average monthly fixed gross salary per employee.
- **Average Annual CTC:** Average annual cost to company.
- **Median Compa-Ratio:** Compensation compared with the salary band midpoint.
- **Average Annual Hike:** Average annual salary hike percentage.
- **Gender Pay Gap:** Level-adjusted comparison of male and female compensation.
- **Employer Cost Growth:** Year-over-year growth in total employer cost.

### 2. Payroll Trend Analysis

The dashboard supports analysis of payroll performance across different months and years.

Key metrics include:
- Monthly employer cost.
- Monthly gross earnings.
- Monthly net pay.
- Payroll expense trends.
- Year-over-year payroll growth.

### 3. Department-Wise Payroll Analysis

This section focuses on understanding payroll expenses across different departments.

Analysis includes:
- Department-wise employer cost.
- Department-wise gross earnings.
- Bonus and incentive expenses.
- Average monthly cost per employee.

### 4. Compensation & Salary Analysis

This section provides insights into employee compensation across different job levels.

Key metrics include:
- Average CTC by job level.
- Male and female average CTC.
- Level-wise pay gap.
- Median compa-ratio.
- Headcount by job level.

### 5. Bonus & Incentive Analysis

The dashboard analyzes different types of employee incentives, including:

- Annual Performance Bonus.
- Joining Bonus.
- Quarterly Sales Incentives.

It helps understand how variable compensation contributes to overall payroll expenses.

## 📈 Key Business Metrics & Calculations

The project uses the following business calculations:

**1. Total Employer Cost**

Total Employer Cost = Gross Earnings + Employer PF + Employer ESI + Gratuity Provision

**2. Net Pay**

Net Pay = Gross Earnings - Total Employee Deductions

**3. Bonus Percentage**

Bonus % = (Bonus / Incentive ÷ Gross Earnings) × 100

**4. Employer Cost Growth**

Growth % = ((Current Year Cost - Previous Year Cost) ÷ Previous Year Cost) × 100

**5. Compa-Ratio**

Compa-Ratio = Employee CTC ÷ Salary Band Midpoint

**6. Gender Pay Gap**

Pay Gap % = ((Male Average CTC - Female Average CTC) ÷ Male Average CTC) × 100

## 🔍 Key Insights – 2025

Based on the dashboard's 2025 selected-year values:

- Total employer cost reached approximately ₹199.25 crore.
- Gross earnings amounted to approximately ₹187.20 crore.
- Net pay was approximately ₹156.16 crore.
- TDS deductions were approximately ₹22.18 crore.
- Statutory and retiral costs reached approximately ₹12.05 crore.
- Bonuses and incentives represented approximately 9.33% of gross earnings.
- Median compa-ratio was approximately 0.979.
- Average annual CTC was approximately ₹15.88 lakh.
- Average annual hike was approximately 10.26%.
- Level-adjusted gender pay gap was approximately 2.93%.

These metrics provide an overview of payroll expenses, employee compensation, and workforce cost trends.
